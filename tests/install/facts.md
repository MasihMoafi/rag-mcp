# Launch notes

The launch codename is Cedar Lantern. The team picked it during the spring planning session because the old name leaked in a public changelog.

## Schedule

The release train leaves on the first Tuesday of the quarter. Feature freeze happens two weeks earlier, and the documentation freeze follows three days after that.

## Ownership

Retrieval quality is owned by the search group. Packaging and installation belong to the platform group, which also maintains the container images and the release checklist.

## Support

Customer reports arrive through the shared inbox and are triaged every morning. Anything touching billing is escalated the same day.
